# M11 P9a: Karma Render Settings, outlines and look-dev overrides

Package P9a of [M11.md](M11.md) (the first part of P9): two image-changing feature groups of the Karma Render
Settings LOP (`karmarendersettings`) that P1 only warned about: **outlines** (toon lines, the 27
`karma:global:outline_*` and `karma:object:outline_*` properties, [karma-properties.md](../research/m11/karma-properties.md)
section 16) and the **look-dev overrides** (`override_lighting`, `disable_lighting`, the headlight's occlusion and
depth cue fog, `autoheadlight`; section 15). Both follow P1's fallback rule
([M11-P1.md](M11-P1.md) 1): Nova has no settings of its own for them, so Karma's authored values are read
directly (status H of the research), on the render settings and, for the per-object outline properties, on prims
(the Render Geometry Settings LOP's Karma tab, inherited primvars too).

All measurements are Karma CPU 22.0.466 through isolated husk (Path Traced, uniform oracle, colour limits off),
the probes of the session's scratch directory (`probe_*.py`); the committed scenes are `tests/husk/karma_looks/`.

## 1. What the LOPs author

- `karmarendersettings` authors all 18 `karma:global:outline_*` at their defaults in every scene (bools, ints,
  floats, `outline_color` float3). With Outlines on it adds the render var `outline` (raw source `outline`,
  color4); with Lines Types to AOVs `outline_contour`, `outline_crease`, `outline_depth`; with Output Debug AOVs
  `export_outline_id`, `export_outline_thickness` (float) and `export_outline_color` (color3).
- Its Simplified Shading toggle (`force_headlight`) authors `karma:global:disable_lighting = 1`. Its Headlight AO
  and fog parameters (`head_ao_samples`, `head_ao_distance` default 1, `head_addfog`, `head_depthcue_*`) author
  nothing (measured with every value changed): they reach Karma only through husk's `--ao-samples`,
  `--ao-distance` or through another LOP.
- `override_lighting`, `autoheadlight` and the `head_*` properties are on the generic Render Settings LOP's Karma
  tab: `override_lighting` as its menu label token (`"Emissive Objects"`), the others as int, float, float2
  (`head_depthcue_z`), float3 and bool. The registry defaults: Off, off, 1 sample, 1e38, fog (0, 0) (off), black,
  alpha 1.
- The Render Geometry Settings LOP authors `primvars:karma:object:outline_*` (9 properties) per prim.

at the image centre), a camera at the origin (0.00225 tan per pixel at 320 x 240), object radius 0.02 (8.9
pixels); folds and steps of one mesh for creases and depth lines.

**Crease detail not reproduced.** On a convex or concave 45 degree fold Karma flags fewer stencil points than the
normal test alone near the fold: the line's mass is 2.30 (left) and 2.32 (right) of 2.83 (every point across),
with dips at 0.56-0.6 of the stencil's world radius from the fold on either face, independent of the sample
count (2, 4, 8, 16) and of the distance (z = -5 and -20), growing with the angle (26: 1.92, 30: 2.04, 37: 2.18,
45: 2.30, 60: 2.47) and with a lower threshold (30 degrees at 0.05: 2.41). The rule behind it was not found (tested:
smoothed normals (Karma's are faceted), a world-distance or slope cut, the plane deviation, ring neighbours).
Nova flags every point across the fold: its crease lines on folds are up to 23 % denser than Karma's (5.70
against 4.67 on the committed 45 degree fold; the step's crease and depth lines and every contour match).

## 4. Nova

### 4.1 Settings (hydra)

`GetNovaSettings` reads the `karma:global:outline_*` into `RenderSettings::outline` (`core/integrator/outline.h`)
and the render settings' `karma:object:outline_*` into `objectOutline` (the defaults of prims without their own,
`Scene::setDefaultOutline`); prims' `primvars:karma:object:outline_*` become `OutlineOverrides`
(`HdNovaObjectOutline`, `Scene::setMeshOutline`) on meshes, curves, points and spheres. `override_lighting` (label
or index), `autoheadlight`, `disable_lighting` and the `head_*` go into `RenderSettings::look`
(`core/integrator/lookdev.h`). `outline_secondary(_samples, _stencils)` and `outline_idthreshold` change nothing
(no secondary lines in Karma; objects are compared exactly), `outline_linesaovs` and `outline_debug` only add
render vars, which Nova fills when present. The P1 warnings for outlines, `disable_lighting` and
`override_lighting` are gone.

The outline render vars map to utility planes (`hydra/aov.cpp`): `outline`, `outline_contour`, `outline_crease`,
`outline_depth`, `export_outline_id`, `export_outline_thickness`, `export_outline_color`, summed per sample and
filtered like the beauty (`utilityPerSample`). As in Karma, lines are only computed when one of the first four
is among the render's outputs (`OutlineSettings::requested`, set by the `Renderer` from the film's planes).

### 4.2 Outlines (CPU and XPU)

The shared header `core/integrator/outline.h` holds the stencil (`outlinePointCount`, `outlineOffset`,
`outlineDirection`, `outlineRadius`), the per-ray test (`outlinePointFlags`), the weight (`outlineWeight`), the
planes' values and the composite. After the camera rays' closest hits (depth 0):

- CPU (`wavefront.cpp traceOutlines`): per camera hit on an object with outlines its `OutlineMain` (point, normal
  facing the ray, rho, object); per stencil point a batch of closest-hit rays from the camera ray's origin
  (camera ray class, its time and opacity seed), resolved and flagged; the sample's `OutlineSample` waits for the
  film, where the beauty takes the composite and the outline planes the values.
- XPU (`kernels.cu novaOutlineMain/Rays/Flags/Finish`): the same per stencil point with the AO ray queue and the
  light-query hit arrays (free at depth 0); `novaFlush` composites and fills the device's outline planes.

The rotations come from `Rng` streams per ring (`stream::outline`), so both devices draw the same stencils.
Per-object outline settings reach the GPU with the mesh (`GpuMesh::outline`).

### 4.3 Look-dev overrides

- Lights (`Scene::setLightOverride`, applied at `commit()`): any mode but Off leaves the stage's lights out of the
  light list (emissive geometry, the emitters after them, stays); Headlight, or `autoheadlight` on a stage
  without visible lights, adds a delta distant light of irradiance 2 along the camera's -Z
  (`kHeadlightIrradiance`) that casts no shadows (`LightData::castsShadows`: its samples' shadow rays have length
  0, which every occlusion query, the fake-caustic pass and the media see as empty; the OptiX raygens skip empty
  segments); Dome Light a white untextured dome of radiance 1. The render pass hands the camera over; a camera
  move rebuilds the light list (the headlight follows it). Both devices get the lights as any others.
- Simplified shading (`shadeSimplified` in `wavefront.cpp`, the same branch in `kernels.cu shadeHit`): at a camera
  hit, after its emission, `simplifiedShade` (display colour from `displayColor`, else `Cd`, else white; the
  facing ratio with the shading normal; the fog over the hit distance) is either added (`head_ao_samples` 0) or
  carried by one cosine-distributed shadow ray of length `head_ao_distance` around the shading normal (one per
  camera sample: Karma's sample count only changes noise); the path ends. Light path expressions get nothing of
  it.

depth blend; simplified shading: facing, fog, occlusion and its distance, emission; the light overrides,

changed light list, the shadow-ray length rule and the OptiX empty-segment guards change nothing there.

## 6. Deviations and open items

- Crease lines on folds: section 2's unexplained thinning near the fold; Nova's crease lines on convex and
  concave folds are up to 23 % denser (mass) than Karma's. Contour, depth and the plane-test creases match.
- One stencil sample (`outline_primary_samples` 1): Karma's single point points towards +x/+y with a per-pixel
  rotation (one-sided lines); Nova rotates it at random per sample (lines on both sides at half the weight).
- The `export_outline_id` values are Nova's own per-object numbers (Karma's are its object hashes); shader exports
  of `outline_color`, `outline_id`, `outline_thickness` (Karma's per-material line colour, id and thickness) are not
  read: the global colour applies.
- Simplified shading: volumes still scatter as in a normal render, light path expressions stay empty, holdouts
  behave as usual (unmeasured in Karma).
- The viewport: Karma computes lines only with an outline AOV; the viewport's beauty alone shows none in Nova
  either.
- Nova's own Render Settings LOP has no outline or look-dev controls (no Nova settings were added: Karma's
  properties are read directly); they come from the Karma Render Settings LOP, the Render Settings LOP's Karma
  tab and the Render Geometry Settings LOP.
- `head_ao_samples` above 1 renders one occlusion ray per camera sample (the same image, Karma's noise level
  differs).
- Outline stencil rays reach past the camera's far clip (as Karma's cone rays do, unmeasured).
