# M11 P2: holdouts and ray visibility (Karma Shadow Catcher, Karma Background Plate)

Package P2 of [M11.md](M11.md): Nova renders scenes made with the Karma Shadow Catcher LOP and the Karma Background
Plate LOP (1.0, 2.0) as Karma does, on CPU and XPU. Research: the Shadow Catcher and Background Plate rows, K2 and the
probes of [karma-lops.md](../research/m11/karma-lops.md), section 7 of [karma-properties.md](../research/m11/karma-properties.md).

## 1. What the nodes author

| Node | On the target prims | Elsewhere |
|---|---|---|
| Shadow Catcher | `primvars:karma:object:holdoutmode` 2 (int), `primvars:karma:object:rendervisibility` "primary" ("primary\|shadow" with Blocks Lighting), a Standard Surface material (specular 0) | - |
| Background Plate 1.0, 2.0 | holdoutmode 2, a material whose base colour is the plate (`ND_image` of a `.pic` at `position` in `space:ndc`, scaled and translated) for rays of level > 0 and grey 0.2 for camera rays; the off-screen colour or a texture looked up with `transformvector` of `ray:direction` from `space:current` to `space:world` | on `/Render/rendersettings`: `karma:global:background_iprfilter` (a "background" image filter: plate, shadows, extras) and `karma:global:use_background` "Auto"; RenderVars `holdout_shadow` (`holdouts;shadow;CDL`) and `holdout_diffuse` (`holdouts;CD.+L`), which the Karma Render Settings LOP adds to its product |
| Karma Render Settings, "Add Holdout Shadows To Beauty" | - | `karma:global:imagefilter` `[["background", {"destination": "C", "foreground": "C", "shadows": "holdout_shadow", "shadowcatcherenable": true, "shadowcatcheralpha": a}]]` and a `__holdout_shadow` var |

## 2. Karma's behaviour (measured)

Isolated husk, Karma CPU 22.0.466, 96 x 96, 256 samples, uniform oracle, box filter; probe scenes as in
`tests/husk/check_karma_nodes.py` (top view: ground, ball, a sun at 45 degrees (normalized, 3), a dome (0.3); a 45
degree mirror onto vertical cards; a wall lit by a grazing sun beside the ground; a background box over part of the
ground). Values are region means.

### 2.1 Camera rays

- **Matte (1) and Background (2) are the same for the camera**: colour 0, alpha 0 (ball and ground, any light). The
  path is shaded as usual and its whole light goes to the `holdout;`/`holdouts;` outputs: `holdout;C.*[LO]` on a matte
  ball 0.4744 = the ball's beauty without holdout (0.4744); every other output is 0 there (`C<RD>L`, `shadow;`,
  `unoccluded;` ...). The utility AOVs (`ray:hitP`) still show the holdout (P.y 1.88 on the ball).
- **No shadow in the beauty's alpha**: a background ground has alpha 0 even in the umbra (0.0000), with the Shadow
  Catcher LOP too. The shadow is in `holdouts;shadow;CDL` (= 1 - lit / unoccluded direct diffuse light on holdouts:
  0.7235 in the umbra under sun and dome, 1.0 with the sun alone, as `shadow;C<RD>L` of an ordinary ground). Only the
  image filter puts it into the alpha (2.4).
- The `holdout;` and `holdouts;` prefixes are the same.

### 2.2 Other rays

- **Matte objects are ordinary objects**: seen in a mirror (0.2840 = the lit card), bouncing light (0.1078 = an
  ordinary wall), casting shadows.
- **Background objects are pre-lit**: a reflection (or a diffuse or refraction ray) sees an emitting surface (an `O`
  event: `C<RD>O`, not in `C<RD>.+L`) of radiance = its **diffuse albedo times the fraction of the light that reaches
  it**, whatever the lights' strength: mirror 0.4957 = 0.5 x the mirror's 0.9913 with the sun at 3 or 1; albedo 0.2:
  0.1983; a Standard Surface with specular 1, roughness 0.3: 0.4855 (the diffuse lobe under the coat, 0.979 of the base
  colour); a metal: 0; the card shadowed from the sun by a ball: 0; with the dome as well: 0.0767 in the sun's shadow,
  0.3880 out of it (= lit / unoccluded irradiance there). A wall beside the ground bounces 0.1125 instead of 0.1078.
- **illumbackground**: a light with it lights background objects as it lights others, on top of the pre-lit radiance
  (mirror 0.7797 = 0.4957 + 0.2840; bounce 0.2208 = 0.1125 + 0.1076); its shadows count neither in the pre-lit fraction
  (shadowed card with an illumbackground sun: 0.4957) nor in `holdouts;shadow;` (0.1027 = the dome's share alone; 0 with
  both lights), while `holdouts;unoccluded;` and `holdout;` keep it (`holdouts;unoccluded;CDL` 0.48804 with and
  without it).
- **The shadow fraction of background points** (2.5): Karma divides a `holdouts;shadow;` output's occluded light by
  the point's *whole* unoccluded direct light, all lobes and all lights that do not illuminate the background, not by
  the output's own. A coated grey (Standard Surface, specular 1, roughness 0.3) background ground in the umbra:
  `holdouts;shadow;CDL` 0.70035 = (0.46739 - 0.12890) / (0.46739 + 0.01592) (its diffuse and glossy unoccluded
  light), `CGL` 0.00742, `C.L` 0.70777 = their sum; the same ground as an ordinary object or a Matte holdout gives
  `shadow;CDL` 0.72422 = 1 - 0.12890 / 0.46739. Base colour 0.2: 0.66625 (the Background Plate's camera colour);
  IOR 4: 0.50080; specular only: `CDL` 0, `CGL` 0.22424. Per light: `CD<L.'sun'>` 0.66925 + `CD<L.'dome'>` 0.03110
  = `CDL`. An illumbackground sun leaves numerator and denominator (0.09605 = the dome-only value).
- **bgoccludeshadow**: background holdouts never shadow background points (a background box over the background
  ground: `holdouts;shadow;` 0 in its shadow, the light 0.3376 kept); with `karma:global:bgoccludeshadow` a background
  point that a background holdout occludes keeps its light even where a CG object shadows it too (0 instead of 0.56).
  For points of ordinary objects background holdouts are ordinary occluders.
- **Ray visibility** (`rendervisibility`): `-primary` hides the ball from the camera only (the ground below it, its
  shadow kept); `-shadow` removes its shadow (the ground under sun and dome 0.4910 instead of 0.1379, also the dome's
  share: Karma tests the light a scattered ray reaches with shadow visibility); `""` hides it from every ray;
  `-reflect` and `-refract` hide it from mirrors and glass, `-diffuse` from bounces (0.0005 left of 0.1078).
- **Light-only rays** (past a ray limit) see no pre-lit plate: a ground bouncing off a background wall with diffuse
  limit 0 gets 0.00047, as with an ordinary or an emissive wall; a mirror with reflect limit 0 shows nothing.

### 2.4a Volumes

A fog box (`volumeuniform`, black absorber or Karma's white medium of density 1, 1.5 wide over the ground) and
`pyro.vdb` (Karma's default volume), sun and dome:

- **rendervisibility** works per ray class as on surfaces: `-primary` (the ground under the box 0.24664: seen
  unattenuated, still in its shadow), `-shadow` (0.10831: seen through, unshadowed; beside the box 0.48146; the dome's
  light reaches the ground through the box as through a shadow ray), `""` (0.48641: gone), `primary` as `-shadow`,
  `-diffuse` (bounces pass), `-volume` changes nothing here.
- **Matte** (1): for the camera the ground behind keeps its attenuated light (0.05512 as without holdout), alpha
  drops to the transmittance (0.2231 = e^-1.5); the camera ray's scattering in the volume goes to `holdout;` outputs
  (white fog: beauty 0.06469 + `holdout;` 0.17612 = the plain beauty 0.24081; `pyro.vdb` 0.28413 + 0.12189 = 0.40602);
  other rays see an ordinary volume, its shadow stays.
- **Background** (2): for the camera as Matte, but its points are lit without background holdouts' shadows, its own
  included (`holdout;` 0.32171 instead of 0.17612; `pyro.vdb` 0.15198 instead of 0.12189); rays other than the
  camera's see it absorb only (no in-scattering: the ground's bounce light as with the black fog, `C.*V.*` 0), shadow
  rays of ordinary points see it whole; background holdouts (surface or volume) never shadow background points.

### 2.3 The textures and spaces of the Background Plate

- `.pic` images are read with their stored values: an 8-bit plate shown as emission equals its codes / 255 (mean
  error 0.004, the sRGB decode 0.08), although Houdini's OCIO file rules give `.pic` no rule.
- `space:current` is world space (Karma shades in world space): `transformvector` current -> world is the identity.
- `space:ndc`: x, y in [0, 1] over the image (y up); the plate shader keeps on-screen points (z > -0.5).

### 2.4 The image filters

- `use_background` "Auto" (the Background Plate's): husk applies no filter (alpha 0 on the ground); the viewport (IPR)
  composites (isolated viewport check, `run_viewport_check.ps1 -Only plate`: Karma's viewport shows the plate, opaque,
  darkened in the ball's shadow). "On": husk composites `C = C + (1 - alpha) (plate (1 - holdout_shadows) +
  holdout_diffuse)`, alpha 1 (the 8-bit plate decoded from sRGB, as `iconvert` linearizes it, unlike the Background
  Plate's own shader lookup, which keeps the stored values (2.3); the texel under the pixel centre: median error
  0.0044, mean 0.011 of 0.098, against 0.0063 / 0.012 bilinear and 0.0098 / 0.018 for the pixel's box average; the
  image's top row is the plate's top row). The viewport shows the same decoded plate. `backgroundfile` is a plain string, relative to the working directory: Karma's viewport, whose
  working directory is elsewhere, shows a flat pink for `./plate.pic`.
- The shadow catcher filter: `alpha = alpha + (1 - alpha) a holdout_shadow`, colour unchanged (0.3620 = 0.5 x 0.7240).

## 3. Design

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| D1 | **Data**: `ObjectVisibility {mask, holdout}` per mesh (`core/scene/visibility.h`), unset fields from the render settings' `karma:object:rendervisibility` / `holdoutmode` (the holdout mode as Karma's menu label there); effective values at `Scene::commit()`. Hydra reads the constant primvars on meshes, spheres, curves and points (`HdNovaObjectVisibility`); a value that does not parse warns once and stays unset. No Nova-named property: none exists in Karma's tab either. |
| D2 | **rendervisibility** parses Karma's menu expressions: ray types primary, shadow, diffuse, reflect, refract, volume, sss, `*`; `-`/`!`/`^` complement, `&`, `\|`, parentheses; whitespace-separated terms as a Houdini pattern (a plain term adds, a negated one removes; a list starting with a negated term starts from all); empty = invisible. The mask has one bit per `RayClass`. |
| D3 | **Visibility in the hit filters**: a mesh with a partial mask is filtered (Embree argument filter, OptiX any-hit; the default costs nothing); the class of the ray (closest hits) or Shadow (occlusion) must be in the mask. First-hit AOVs and cryptomattes come from camera rays and follow the camera bit. |
| D4 | **Light along scattered rays with shadow visibility** (Karma, 2.2 `-shadow`): when some object's shadow bit differs from another scattered class's (`Scene::shadowVisibilityDiffers`), each scattered ray is traced a second time as a shadow ray (closest hit) and the lights it collects (area lights crossed, lights at infinity on escape) end there; the ray's own hit is shaded as before. |
| D5 | **Camera hits on holdouts** (`core/integrator/holdout.h`): the path becomes a holdout path: alpha 0 (the volumes in front keep theirs), its light leaves the beauty (`addRadiance`) and its LPE state switches to the holdout automaton. Without holdout outputs (and no AO) the path ends there. |
| D6 | **holdout; outputs** through the automaton: an internal event `TypeHoldout` (no syntax) is put before the camera event of `holdout;`/`holdouts;` expressions; `.` and negations never match it, so other expressions never accept a holdout path and holdout expressions only those. `Program::holdoutStart` = the state after the holdout and camera events; the switch happens at the camera ray's first surface, where the state is still the start. The outputs feed the plain, unoccluded and lit sums like others: `holdouts;shadow;CDL` is 1 - lit / unoccluded over holdout paths. Nothing else in the LPE code changes. |
| D7 | **Background points' shadow rays** (camera hits on background holdouts, pre-lit fractions) carry `kRayFromBackground` in the ray's class byte: the filter lets them pass background holdouts (all background meshes are filtered). bgoccludeshadow: their occluded rays are traced again against background holdouts alone (`kRayProbeBackground`; every mesh is filtered while it may run); a hit makes them unoccluded. |
| D8 | **Pre-lit background objects** for every ray but the camera's (and not for light-only rays, as emission): radiance = the closure's diffuse albedo (reflected albedo of its diffuse lobes) times the visibility of one shadow ray chosen by resampled importance among 4 light samples and 4 cosine directions towards the lights at infinity (MIS between them), each weighted by its unoccluded diffuse contribution; over the candidates this is lit / unoccluded light. An O event; the path ends. Where no candidate carries light (faces turned away) the plate is black, as Karma's (a box face away from the sun: 0). A picked illumbackground light counts as reaching the point (no ray); those lights add their own light by next-event estimation of weight 1. |
| D9 | **illumbackground on camera hits**: by D14 their light samples at a background point feed `unoccluded;` outputs (at the shading point, independent of the shadow ray) and leave `shadow;` outputs and the holdout total out, as Karma; a continuation's lit sum from such a point leaves them out too. |
| D10 | **Spaces**: `space:current` = world, `space:world`, `space:object`/`model` in `transformvector`/`point`/`normal` and `position`/`normal`; `position` in `space:ndc` reads a new global `Pndc` (the render camera's projection in `ShadeGlobals`, CPU and GPU). Unknown spaces warn once. |
| D11 | **.pic** images go through imaketx as `.rat` files do, always with their stored values; their automatic colour space is none. |
| D12 | **Image filters**: the shadow catcher filter (`imagefilter` "background" with `shadowcatcherenable`) is applied to the bound beauty from the bound shadow AOV; the plate composite by D16. |
| D13 | **CPU and XPU identical**: the decisions in `holdout.h` (state switch, diffuse albedo, the candidate reservoir) are shared; the kernels mirror the CPU passes; the pre-lit plate's illumbackground shadow ray uses the walk exits' shadow kind (a path has no walk at that depth); the bgoccludeshadow probe is an extra occlusion launch over the shadow queue. A program whose outputs are all `holdout;` ones has a dead start state (0): the switch to `holdoutStart` happens from it too (the XPU skipped it before, leaving such outputs black). |
| D14 | **Background points' shadow fraction** (Karma, 2.2): a holdout `shadow;` output of a background point is (its unoccluded - its lit light) / the point's whole unoccluded light. Per light sample (and per continuation towards the lights at infinity light sampling never picks) at a background point, `lpeDepositBackgroundUnit` (`lpe_path.h`) gives `unoccluded;` outputs their light as anywhere and `shadow;` outputs only their occluded part, as a negative lit sum; the sample's whole weight-1 light (all lobes: the closure's value, the continuation's weight before the class choice) goes to one extra lit slot, `Compiled::holdoutTotalSlot()`, which the resolve adds to both sums of every holdout `shadow;` output: `1 - (L + B) / (U + B)` with U and L the other points' sums and L also holding minus the background points' occluded light, i.e. (their occluded light + the background points') over (their unoccluded light + the background points' whole light). Matte points and every other vertex keep the per-output rule, so a pixel mixing both stays a ratio of sums. illumbackground lights: D9. Shadow-linked domes' own continuation rays keep the per-output rule. |
| D15 | **Volumes** (2.4a): a volume (VDB or a P5 shell) takes `rendervisibility` and `holdoutmode` like a surface (`VolumeRecord`, the same render-settings defaults; `MediumVolume::visibility/holdout` for the shared trackers). The trackers drop volumes a ray's class does not see (`mediumSees`); lights gathered along scattered rays see them with shadow visibility where it differs (`Scene::volumeShadowVisibilityDiffers`: the light transmittance of `lightSplit`, D4's counterpart). Camera rays: an event in a holdout volume makes the path a holdout path (a Background one a background point: kRayFromBackground, D14), holdout volumes' emission goes to `holdout;` outputs, alpha = the other volumes' opacity (flyMedia's `alphaV`: alphaT times their share of the majorant at each collision) + what lies behind (1 opaque, 0 holdout or nothing) times the whole transmittance, and equiangular sampling is off for them (the free flight's events alone, weight 1). Rays other than the camera's see Background volumes absorb only (`mediumAbsorbsOnly`: their scattering becomes absorption, no events, no emission); shadow rays from background points skip Background volumes (`MediumRay::fromBackground`), and so does the light a ray leaving a background point gathers (the split light transmittance at depth 1, and light-only continuations: a constant dome is found by the continuation alone; without it Nova's white background fog read 0.255 against Karma's 0.322, with it 0.321). |
| D16 | **The plate composite** (2.4): the "background" filter of `karma:global:imagefilter` with a `backgroundfile`, or `background_iprfilter` where `use_background` is On (husk) or Auto in the viewport, applies `C + (1 - alpha) (plate (1 - shadows) + extras)`, alpha 1, to the bound destination when the film resolves (`renderBuffer.cpp`): `shadows` and each of `extras` are bound AOVs by name (the Background Plate's vars; the viewport binds them too), the plate (`HdNovaLoadPlate`: `.pic`/`.rat` through imaketx, stored values; relative paths from the working directory, as Karma) is read once per file version, placed as the Background Plate's shader places it (`(ndc - 0.5 - translate) / scale + 0.5`), the texel under the pixel centre, black off the image; an integer image's stored values decoded from sRGB (`.pic`, `.rat`: 8-bit; float images as they are; with stored values Nova's viewport showed the sRGB encoding of Karma's, region means 0.45 / 0.53 / 0.53 against 0.17 / 0.28 / 0.30, decoded 0.173 / 0.277 / 0.297). A plate that cannot be read warns and composites black. The holdout total of D14 counts every light sample and continuation of a background point, also where the sampled lobe's or event's state leads to no output (the Background Plate's AOVs name no glossy path: without that its umbra read 0.716). |

## 4. Verification

- `tests/test_holdouts.cpp`: the expression parser (Karma's menu entries and patterns); the holdout automaton (outputs
  of holdout and ordinary paths); visibility (`-primary`, `-shadow`, `""`, `-reflect`, render-settings defaults, light
  through `-shadow` objects and blocked by shadow-only ones under a constant dome); Matte and Background camera hits
  (alpha, beauty, `holdout;`, the shadow fraction 1 / 0 analytic); pre-lit cards in a mirror (albedo x reflectance, 0 in
  a shadow, illumbackground added); bgoccludeshadow off and on; background points' shadow fraction over their whole
  light (a coated ground's diffuse and glossy shadows add up to 1, a Matte's are 1 each, illumbackground lights kept in
  `unoccluded;`); volumes (analytic: Matte and Background alpha e^-1 and the ground behind unchanged, `-primary`,
  `-shadow`, `""`, background volumes leaving background points unshadowed, single scattering of holdout volumes into
  `holdout;` with and without their own shadow); `[gpu]` CPU vs XPU for all of them.
- `tests/test_hydra_holdouts.cpp`: primvars on meshes, spheres, curves, points and media (int, token labels, invalid
  expressions), edits, render-settings defaults, bgoccludeshadow, illumbackground, the background filter's JSON
  (file, scale, translate, extras), the plate's placement and the viewport's decode. `tests/test_hydra_karma.cpp`:
  Karma's spaces. `tests/test_hydra_textures.cpp`: `.pic` stored values.
- `tests/husk/check_karma_nodes.py` (ctest `husk_karma_nodes`): the probes (now also coated holdouts, a light's share,
  illumbackground in `unoccluded;`, light-only rays, fog boxes and `pyro.vdb` with each mode and visibility) and the
  LOP scenes (`tests/husk/karma_nodes`, rebuilt by `build_scenes.py`; the Background Plate's `holdout_shadows` umbra
  0.666 back in; its scene with `use_background` On, the composite) against Karma's values (3 % + 2e-3; alpha exact).
- `tests/houdini/run_viewport_check.ps1 -Only plate`: the Background Plate's scene (a perspective camera of the
  viewport's aspect over it) in Karma CPU, Nova CPU and Nova XPU; the plate shows composited in all three.
- Golden: scenes without these primvars stay bit-identical.
