# M11 P8: Karma Texture Baker

Package P8 of [M11](M11.md): Nova bakes the textures Karma's Texture Baker LOP (`karmatexturebaker`) asks for.

## 1. What the LOP authors

`husd/karmatexturebaker.py` (read for its behaviour, nothing copied) builds, for the first UDIM tile of the bake
mesh, a RenderSettings prim `/Render/karmabake_<UDIM>` (one more prim per further tile, referencing the first and
overriding `karma:global:bake_udim` and the product names):

- `karma:global:bake_*`: mode (`trace`, `uvmatch`, `single`), `bake_low` (a prim path; its meshes and those below
  it), cage mode and path, `bake_high` (UV Match only; empty in the other modes), UV primvar, UDIM, MikkT switch,
  trace bias and maximum distance (global, per-point attribute or texture), skew texture, trace set, the parameters
  of every map (`oc`, `cu`, `cv`, `th`, `eg`, `rn`), the position box (`pbox_mode`, `uniform`, `min`, `max`) and
  `bake_use_lighting`;
- `karma:global:samplesperpixel` (9), `autoraybias` 0, `disableMotionBlur`, dicing settings;
- one raw RenderVar per map: `Nt`, `Ds`, `Vd`, `Vdt`, `Oc`, `Cv`, `Th`, `Cu`, `Eg`, `Rn`, `Af`, `__bake_P`, `N`,
  `Ng`, `export_<input>` for the Standard Surface inputs; with lighting the LPEs `C.*[LO]`, `C<RD>.*L`, ... and
  `ray:hitN_facingratio`;
- one product per var and UDIM, named `<picture base><separator><var>.<format>` by the LOP (husk writes them;
  nothing for the renderer to name);
- `karma:global:imagefilter`: `shift` filters (the [-1, 1] to [0, 1] remap of `Nt`/`Rn` and `N`/`Ng`, the "green
  up" flip, the world-space swizzles), `uv_enlarger` (`diffusefill`, 4 border pixels, alpha `Af`) and optionally
  `denoise`;
- the camera `/__HoudiniTextureBakeCamera` framing the bake mesh (only for dicing);
- `primvars:karma:object:rendervisibility = ""` on the low mesh (and on every other gprim when a high mesh is
  named in Trace mode).

## 2. Karma measured

Isolated husk with Karma CPU (Houdini 22.0.466) on hand-built bake scenes (scratch probes p1-p17; the regression
scenes are `tests/husk/karma_bake/make_scenes.py`).

**Texels and samples.** The film is the UDIM tile's texture space (row 0 at the top is v = 1). Every pixel sample
is placed by Karma's default pixel filter (Gaussian, sigma 0.5 px, filter importance sampling, as camera renders):
the sample's (u, v) finds the low mesh's triangle; a sample outside every triangle, or whose rays find nothing,
adds zero. So **every map is the coverage-weighted mean** (premultiplied): `Af` is the fraction of samples with a
shading point (0.842 / 0.158 / 0.001 next to an island border at 1024 samples, the Gaussian's 0.841 / 0.159 /
0.001; 0.707 at a corner), `Oc` is 0.889 where `Af` is 0.889 on open ground, and so on.

**Texture space on quads.** Karma maps texture space onto a quad through the triangles of its second-to-fourth
corner diagonal, whichever winding and whichever diagonal is shorter (a skewed trapezoid with square UVs: P.x
0.5638 at (0.3125, 0.3125), the 1-3 split gives 0.5625, the 0-2 split 0.375, bilinear 0.684); Hydra's fan splits
along 0-2.

**Seams.** Where two UV islands touch but lie apart in space, Karma's ray-query maps darken in the texels whose
filter mixes them (occlusion 0.55 instead of 1, curvature 0.40 instead of 0.5, edge 0 and noise in `Rn`); the
covered texels away from such seams are unaffected. A Karma artefact (presumably its ray offsets from the pixel's
position derivatives); Nova does not reproduce it and the regression scenes keep their islands apart.

**Trace mode.** Two rays per sample along the low mesh's interpolated shading normal N: from P + bias N towards
-N and from P - bias N towards +N, each up to the maximum distance measured from its own origin; the hit with
the smaller ray distance wins (planes at +0.3 and -0.2: the one below; bias 0.02 with planes at +0.01 and -0.005:
+0.01). No hit: the sample adds nothing (`Af` 0). The low normal's tilt moves the shading point
(`Vd` = 0.2236 N for a plane 0.2 above). Cage offset: one ray from P + bias N towards -N (help, Nova follows).

**Tangent frame (`Nt`, `Rn`, `Vdt`).** Components along (T, B, N) of the low point, N its shading normal.
MikkTSpace (`bake_use_mikkt` 1): B = sign cross(N, T) with the sign of the *authored* winding's texture area, so
a left-handed (Houdini) face with standard UVs gets B = -dP/dv; mirrored UVs flip T. Without MikkT: T along dP/du,
B along dP/dv for every winding. `Nt` uses the high surface's shading normal as authored (a plane facing away
still reads (0, 0, -1)). `Ds` = |Vd|, unsigned; `Vd` the world vector from the low point to the shading point,
`Vdt` the same in the frame.

**Position (`__bake_P`).** The shading point; boxes: `bakemesh` the low meshes' bounds, `scene` the visible
geometry's (the hidden low mesh is not in it), `user` the given box; uniform: a cube of the largest extent around
the box's centre; values clamped to [0, 1]; a flat axis maps points above it to 1, on it to 0.

**`N`, `Ng`.** The shading point's normals as authored. Karma's `Ng` is not normalized (a quad of edge 3 reads
(-1.8, -0.9, 9): its parametric cross product); Nova writes the unit geometric normal (deviation).

**Occlusion (`Oc`).** Cosine-weighted directions around N; the fraction not blocked within `oc_maxdist`
(unlimited when negative). Under a ceiling at distance 1 with maximum distance 2: 0.25 (= P(cos < 0.5)).

**Cavity (`Cv`).** Uniform directions around -N (into the surface) from 0.001 below it; the fraction that does
not hit within `cv_maxdist` (slabs of thickness h: (h - 0.001) / d, measured 0.2207, 0.4192, 0.6196, 0.8215 for
h = 0.012..0.042, d 0.05); `cv_bias` blends towards 1 (bias 0.5: 0.5 + 0.5 Cv).

**Curvature (`Cu`).** Convex: uniform directions around -N from P - 0.002 N, per ray max(0, 1 - t / d)
(d = `cu_sdist`), averaged; concave: the same around +N from P + 0.002 N; c = convex - concave;
Cu = 0.5 + 0.5 sign(c) schlickBias(min(1, scale |c|), bias), Schlick's bias function x / ((1/b - 2)(1 - x) + 1).
On slabs the bias-0.5 response is (1 - a) + a ln a with a = (h - 0.002) / d to 4 digits at every (h, d) measured;
bias 0.75 matches Schlick's function to 1e-4, scale 2 likewise; bias 0 gives 0.5, bias 1 saturates. The 0.002
offset does not depend on the low-high gap or the trace bias.

**Thickness (`Th`).** Cosine-weighted directions around -N; the mean distance to the first hit, misses adding
zero (a sphere of radius R reads about (2/3) 2R, a slab of thickness h about 2h; heavy-tailed). Karma's map is far
noisier per texel than its sample count suggests (on the torus: per-texel standard deviation 0.32 on a mean of
0.76); the means agree (0.757 Karma, 0.769 Nova).

**Rounded normal (`Rn`) and edge (`Eg`).** The rounded-edge normal at the shading point, in the tangent frame;
profiles across 90 and 135 degree edges within the radius. The profile is wider than `kma_roundededge`'s (M4.4):
of the weights (1 - (d/r)^2)^k over the surface within the radius, k = 0.5 fits both edges within 0.03 in the
tangential component (k = 1, the M4.4 query's: up to 0.056). Concave edges of one mesh tilt away from the other
face. `Eg` = the other surfaces' weight over the point's own surface's weight (tan of the rounded angle at a
90-degree edge: 0.844 at the edge; 0.667 at a 135-degree edge where the angle is only 17.7 degrees).

**Shader exports (`export_*`).** The Standard Surface inputs at the shading point; unbound geometry bakes Karma's
default Standard Surface (base colour 0.8).

**Lighting.** The beauty and LPEs at the shading point, seen from its front (a plane facing up is lit from above
whether the low point is above or below it; one facing down is dark): Karma's default Standard Surface reflects the
light's highlight along the normal.

**Image filters.** `shift`: v' = swizzle(v) scale + offset, with `ispremultipliedalpha` the offset times the
coverage. `uv_enlarger`: covered texels divided by their coverage (exact), the empty ones filled: `borderpixels`
rings close to the island, a smooth fill beyond (the whole tile is filled). Filters with `runlast` run after the
others.

**Single mode.** The shading point is the low point itself (no rays; `Ds` 0). On an isolated quad: `Oc` 1, `Cu`
0.5; on Karma's lat-long spheres single mode reads occluded where trace mode does not (not reproduced).

## 3. Design

- **Core, `core/bake/`.** `Baker` prepares the bake meshes in world space (their first instance at the frame):
  triangles with positions, normals, texture coordinates, per-point bias/maximum distance and tangent frames, and a
  grid over the tile's texture space. `generate()` turns a wave's pixel samples (the pixel filter's offsets, the
  render's Sobol sampler) into low points and shading points (trace rays as measured; cage offset, topology and UV
  cages; UV Match through the high meshes' own grid; Single), `accumulate()` adds the maps to the film's utility
  planes, which in a bake hold per-sample sums (new `UtilityKind::Bake*` kinds; the plain utilities, primvars and
  facing ratios accumulate per sample too).
- **MikkTSpace** (`core/bake/mikktspace.*`): Nova's implementation of the published algorithm (welding, quads split
  along the shorter texture diagonal, groups of same-orientation triangles around a vertex, angle-weighted
  projected derivatives). The triangulated mesh's quads are rebuilt from `MeshDesc::triangleFaces`;
  `MeshDesc::leftHanded` restores the authored winding's sign. In Trace mode the bake surface then splits each
  quad along its 1-3 diagonal as Karma does (Single and UV Match keep the host's triangles: their shading points
  are resolved on them).
- **Maps** (`Baker::accumulate`): as measured in section 2, each query batched over the wave's samples.
- **Renderer.** `RenderSettings::bake` switches `Renderer::renderPass` to `bakePass`: one sample per pixel and
  pass, tiles in parallel; with `bake_use_lighting` the wavefront runs with the bake's samples as camera rays
  (their first hits injected, `renderWave(..., baker)`), so the beauty and LPEs follow the normal path.
- **Hydra.** `hydra/bake.*` reads the `karma:global:bake_*` settings (meshes by path through a registry the mesh
  rprims fill), warns once for what is unsupported, and runs the `shift` and `uv_enlarger` filters on the bound
  buffers after each copy (coverage = the film's alpha). AOV resolution in a bake maps the baker's raw names to the
  bake kinds; the bake's sample count is `karma:global:samplesperpixel` while `nova:samples` is at its default.
- **XPU.** Bakes render on the CPU (a once-per-process message "Nova XPU bakes textures on the CPU"): a bake is a
  few million rays, the camera path is not involved, and the GPU kernels would need the bake's own ray generation
  and queries. Lighting bakes use the CPU wavefront as well.

## 4. Deviations and limits

- `Ng` is the unit geometric normal (Karma's is unnormalized).
- The island fill beyond the covered texels follows Karma's idea (rings, then a smooth fill) but not its exact
  weights; covered texels are exact.
- Trace sets (`bake_traceset`, `baker_*_traceset` primvars), the bias/maximum-distance/skew textures and the
  `denoise` filter on the maps are not supported (warned); bias and maximum distance attributes are.
- Karma's single mode on curved meshes (see section 2) is not reproduced.
- Rounded edges across two separate objects at a concave corner: Karma tilts towards the other object, Nova away
  from it (as for one connected mesh, where both agree).

## 5. Verification

- `nova_tests "[bake]"`: MikkTSpace (quads, mirrored, reversed winding, angle weighting on a cylinder), Schlick's
  bias against Karma's measured pairs, the filters, the texture-space lookup with UDIM tiles, a trace bake against
  the analytic plane (`Nt`, `Vd`, `Ds`, `__bake_P`, `Oc`, `Cu`, export, coverage).
- `husk_karma_bake` (`tests/husk/check_karma_bake.py`): every scene of section 2 against Karma's stored block
  means, CPU and Nova XPU; the filters against Nova's own raw maps. Worst blocks: tangent normal 0.018 (coverage at
  island borders), height and vector displacement 0.004, position 0.009, occlusion 0.033, cavity 0.012, curvature
  0.009, thickness 20 % of one noisy block (whole-map means 0.757 Karma, 0.769 Nova), rounded normal 0.035, edge
  0.15 at the edge itself (the ratio amplifies the rounded normal's difference), base colour 0.002. On the torus the
  per-texel mean differences are 0.015 (Nt x), 0.025 (Oc), 0.021 (Cu), 0.024 (Cv), 0.0006 (Ds).
- `houdini_karma_texture_baker` (`tests/houdini/test_karma_texture_baker.py`): the LOP itself (a box over two UDIM
  tiles baking a subdivided box) still authors the settings Nova reads; husk + Nova writes both tiles' PNG
  products with remapped, filled normal maps. Against Karma on the same file: mean Nt (0.4993, 0.5004, 0.8474)
  vs Karma's (0.4993, 0.5004, 0.8460), per-texel difference 0.033 at 9 samples; the texels outside the islands
  differ (Karma's fill leaves some at zero there).
- Camera renders are unchanged: the golden check (34 reference scenes, nova_cli) is bit-identical except the four
  scenes M11 P1 changes on purpose (`internalreflectenable`), which are bit-identical too with
  `nova:object:internalReflection` on, as P1 documents.
- Lighting bakes (probe): the front-side view is Karma's (a plane facing down is dark when lit from above, whichever
  side the low point is on); unbound geometry shades with Nova's default material (Lambert 0.18), Karma's baker with
  a default Standard Surface (the M3 decision Q1 difference).
