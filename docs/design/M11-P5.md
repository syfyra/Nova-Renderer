# M11 P5: Karma Fog Box, media bounded by surfaces

Package P5 of [M11](M11.md): the Karma Fog Box LOP, and with it M8's open decision "mesh-bounded volumes"
([M8](M8.md) 2.2, 7): a gprim whose material is a volume becomes a medium bounded by its surface.

## 1. What the Fog Box authors (Houdini 22.0.466)

Measured with the LOP in an isolated hython (`tests/husk/fogbox_scenes.py` builds every test scene with it):

- An Xform at `primpath` (default `/fogbox`, kind component) with `geo/guide` (a guide-purpose BasisCurves outline)
  and `geo/render`, a render-purpose Scope carrying the material binding and three constant primvars, which
  its child gprim `geo/render/volume` inherits: `bool[] karma:object:volumeuniform = [1]`,
  `float[] karma:object:volumeuniformdensity = [<density>]`, `float[] karma:object:volumesteprate = [0.02]`.
  One-element arrays, not scalars.
- The gprim: Box a `Cube` (size 1), Sphere a `Sphere` (radius 0.5), Tube a `Cylinder_1`, Cone a `Cone`, Capsule a
  `Capsule_1`, Torus a `Mesh`, Custom a mesh from a SOP. Translate/rotate/scale go on the Xform.
- The material `mtl/fogboxvolume` (a Karma Material Builder, `outputs:kma:volume` only): `kma_volume` with
  scattering = d * color, absorption = d - d * color (so extinction d, albedo color), anisotropy = phase;
  d = clouddensity * geomprop `density` (default 1), times `remap(fractal3d(ray:hitP * freq / scale + offset),
  -0.5..0.5 -> min..max)` with Add Noise, times `shadowscale` on shadow rays (`ray:isshadowray` switch).
- **With Add Noise the LOP authors no gprim**: `geo/render/volume/volume_0` is a `Volume` prim whose
  `HoudiniFieldAsset` density is an in-memory SOP volume of the box (`op:/stage/<lop>/boxsop/sopnet/OUT.sop.volumes`,
  extent 1.08 for the unit box), with `volumeuniform = [0]` and a step rate scaled to the box. That is an M8
  volume (the USD Render ROP saves the field to a file; a stage exported from the LOP alone cannot resolve the
  `op:` path in husk, for Karma as for Nova, which warns). `fogbox_scenes.py` saves the field next to the scene.

## 3. Decisions

| # | Question | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | Which gprims are media | A mesh (implicit cube, cylinder, cone, capsule, plane through the implicit-surface scene index, or any mesh) or an analytic sphere is a medium when (a) its material has a volume shader and `volumeuniform` is on (the prim's constant primvar `karma:object:volumeuniform`, inherited, scalar or array, or `karma:volumeuniform` on the volume terminal's shader; a surface terminal beside it is ignored, as Karma), (b) its material has a volume shader and no surface terminal (Karma CPU renders nothing there, Karma XPU a volume: Nova renders the material's medium), or (c) `volumeuniform` is on and no material is bound (Karma's white medium of density 1). A prim with a surface-only material stays a surface. `HdNovaIsMedium` (`hydra/medium.{h,cpp}`) decides; the material sprim publishes its kind (surface terminal, volume shader, `karma:volumeuniform`) in the registry, whose change re-syncs the bound prims, so a material edit turns a surface into a medium and back. |
| F2 | Representation | **A scene volume with a shell** (`VolumeDesc::shell`, `core/volume/shell.h`): the gprim leaves the acceleration structure (the shell is invisible to every ray: camera, shadow, light-only, AO, first-hit AOVs, cryptomatte) and its inside is a volume of the material's volume program, tracked by M8's unchanged trackers. The shell is the triangulated control mesh (subdivision surfaces: their hull) at the shutter's middle step, with a BVH of its own (median split, 4 triangles per leaf), or an analytic sphere; in the volume's object space, so transform motion is the volume's (D17). |
| F3 | Entry/exit bookkeeping | **No medium stack: the even-odd rule along each ray.** A ray's spans inside a closed shell are where an odd number of the shell's crossings lie ahead (`shellIntervals` in `shell.h`, called through `shellSpans` by `volumeSpans` for every ray the trackers see). The camera inside a box, shadow rays that start or end inside, rays from surfaces inside, refracted paths, nested and overlapping shells (and shells with VDB volumes: `MediaWalker` adds their majorants and coefficients, M8 D8) need no state. Independent of the surface's orientation (flipped normals change nothing). Crossings come from a watertight ray-triangle test (Woop, Benthin and Wald 2013) with a fill rule for rays through edges and corners: one of the triangles sharing an edge counts the crossing (the edge in winding order turned to face the ray: neighbours traverse it in opposite directions; at a silhouette both or neither count). The state at a span's start comes from all crossings that decide it (closed: the parity of those ahead; open: the sides over the whole line); the crossings within the span are taken 32 at a time, a window ending before the distance of its last kept crossing and the next starting there, so any number of crossings is exact (M11 review; before, the 33rd and later crossings were lost: the 17th of 17 fog spheres in one mesh). |
| F4 | Non-closed meshes | Defined, with a warning once per prim (edges not shared by two triangles, counted at build). A line through a hole crosses the shell an odd number of times, so parity means nothing there (it would fill the line from the ray origin, or not, by direction). Open shells follow the faces' winding over the whole line instead: a point is inside if the faces ahead of it hold more exits (crossed along the winding normal) than entries, or the faces behind it more entries than exits. Holes then behave as closed from every direction (an open box is the box, a bowl is filled to its rim plane), independent of the ray's origin; for closed outward meshes it equals the even-odd rule. It needs outward winding (USD's orientation is applied by the triangulation); closed shells stay orientation-free. Karma renders an open-front box as nothing and an open-side box with a varying density; neither is a definition to copy. |
| F5 | Majorants | The shell's object-space bounds are the domain: one cell when the shader does not read the position (a homogeneous medium: the interval bound is the exact extinction, which ratio tracking then turns into the exact binary transmittance estimator, and free flights into exact exponential flights), else up to 32 cells along the longest side, each bounded by the M8.1 interval run of the shader over the cell's world box. New interval rules for `Noise` and `Fractal` (noise.h's Perlin noise: \|value\| <= 2 x 0.6616 in 2D, 1.5 x 0.9820 in 3D, proven from the fade-weighted corner offsets; fBm: the sum of \|diminish\|^i over the octaves), so noise in a shell, and the Fog Box's noise volume (an M8 volume, section 1), are bounded without estimates or violations (before, the noise volume fell back to the sampled estimate with a warning). `RayType` spans every class, so the bound covers the larger of the camera and shadow densities. Emitting cells keep M8's collision floor with 1/32 of the longest side as the "voxel". |
| F6 | Shader inputs | The volume program as for VDB volumes (D4, D15): P = `ray:hitP` the world point, Pobject the object point, `RayType` shadow on transmittance queries (`ray:isshadowray`), `RayDepth` the level. Primvar reads: no fields; a constant primvar of the gprim with the read's name (`VolumeDesc::constants`, scalar broadcast), else the read's fallback (the Fog Box's `density` geomprop: 1). Bindings encode constants as -2 - i. |
| F7 | Shadow rays, light-only rays, alpha | Unchanged: they track the spans of their segment (D9, D12); a shadow ray from inside the box to a light outside crosses one wall. |
| F8 | Volume lights | `treat_as_lightsource` on a medium works as for VDB volumes (D13); a light sample that lands outside the shell (its cells reach past it) contributes nothing (`shellContains`: the shell's rule along a fixed direction), so the light-sampled and collision estimates agree. |
| F9 | Motion blur | Transform motion of the shell is exact (rays enter object space at their time; bounds over the shutter as D17). A deforming shell keeps its shape at the shutter's middle (a warning once). Instanced media are not supported (a warning; nothing renders). |
| F10 | Ignored settings | `volumeuniformdensity` (no effect in Karma, measured), `volumesteprate` (Nova does not march, M8 D16), `volumeuniformsamples`. No warnings (documented no-ops, as M8). |
| F11 | CPU and XPU | One code path: `shell.h` and the span logic are NOVA_INLINE, the GPU uploads the shell's triangles and nodes with the volume (`MediumVolume::shell`) and the constants (`GpuVolume::constants`). |

## 4. Deviations from Karma

| Case | Karma CPU 22.0.466 | Nova |
|---|---|---|
| volume-only material on a mesh without `volumeuniform` | renders nothing | the medium |
| absorbing uniform emitters | +23 % emission | exact |
| the Fog Box's noise volume at the LOP's step rate | marched (absorbing box 10 % bright) | unbiased (Karma at step rate 1 agrees within 0.5 %) |
| non-closed meshes | front-face entry; open sides vary | holes closed, by the faces' winding over the whole line; warning |
| the Sphere shape | tessellated (alpha through the middle 1 % low: 0.3854 against the exact 0.3894) | the exact sphere |
| first-hit AOVs over fog | the sampled volume event | the first surface (M8 D12's deviation) |

## 6. Verification

See section 7 for the numbers.

- `nova_tests "[shell]"` (`tests/test_volume_shell.cpp`): crossings through faces, shared edges, box edges and
  corners counted once; BVH = brute force on 2000 random rays through a 9024-triangle UV sphere (seam and poles welded), odd inside;
  spans for the camera inside, shadow rays ending inside, two components of one mesh, a transformed shell, an
  open box; alpha 1 - e^-1 through a unit box; the camera inside a sphere seeing a dome through e^-1; the
  default shell medium; constant primvars against the fallback; nested shells adding; the shadow density
  (e^-2 at twice the density); the Fog Box's noise in a sphere: ratio tracking against a quadrature of the
  shader (z-score), no estimated cells, no majorant violations; a volume light inside its shell only.
- `nova_tests "[gpu]"`: "XPU media bounded by surfaces" (a fog box with the shadow switch, a noise sphere, an
  emitting box as a light, all inside a fog box around the camera) against the CPU.
- ctest `husk_karma_fogbox` (`tests/husk/check_karma_fogbox.py`): the Fog Box LOP's scenes through husk with Nova
  and Nova XPU against Karma's values and the analytic transmittance.
- Reference scene `fogbox` (`tests/reference/scenes/fogbox.usda`, written by `fogbox_scenes.py --reference`: a
  white box around a grey block, a coloured box tilted by 35/20 degrees, a dark box with Shadow Density 0, on a
  ground under a key light; Karma reference via `make_references.py`, 2 x 2048 samples). No Sphere shape: Karma
  tessellates it (section 4), and the first reference with one failed on the sphere's silhouette alone.
- The golden check: the reference scenes rendered before are bit-identical.
