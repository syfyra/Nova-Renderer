# M11 P9b: nested dielectrics and light portals

Package P9 of [M11.md](M11.md), its second part: two image-changing object properties that the Karma LOPs author
and that Nova only warned about after P1 ([M11-P1.md](M11-P1.md) section 4):

- `karma:object:dielectricpriority`: the priority of overlapping refracting objects (liquid in a glass);
- `karma:object:isportal`, `karma:object:portaldomelights` and the dome light's `inputs:karma:light:portalmisbias`:
  objects that become light portals for dome lights.

Neither is a parameter of the Karma Render Settings LOP (`karmarendersettings` has none of them): the Render Geometry
Settings LOP's Karma tab authors them as constant primvars, the Dome Light LOP's Karma tab the MIS bias. A Karma value
on the render settings prim (P1's warning looked there) is the default of every object, as for P1's object
properties. All measurements: Karma CPU 22.0.466 through isolated husk, the scenes of `tests/husk/karma_p9b/`
(written by `build_karma_p9b.py` from the real LOPs), 256 Path Traced samples unless noted.

## 1. Nested dielectrics

### 1.1 Karma, measured

Two smooth Standard Surface glasses with absorbing interiors: A (index 1.5, `transmission_color` (1, 0.5, 1) at
`transmission_depth` 1, i.e. green halves per unit) and B (index 2, blue halves per unit), seen straight down by an
orthographic camera over an emitter of radiance 1. At normal incidence nothing bends, so red is the product of the
interfaces' transmission (1 - F: 0.96 for A, 8/9 for B against air), green and blue are 0.5^length inside A and B.
Karma's default `internalreflectenable` off makes exits lossless (P1 3.3). *overlap*: A (z 0..2) over B (z -1..1);
*contain*: B (z 0..1) inside A (z -1..2). Priorities written (A, B); none authored = equal.

| Case | Karma R, G, B | read as |
|---|---|---|
| overlap, equal (none, 0/0, 1/1) | 0.85333, 0.42666, 0.42666 | 0.96 x 8/9; A 1 unit, B 1 unit: entering B replaces A, leaving A leaves no medium |
| overlap A 0, B 1 | 0.96065, 0.24019, 0.48032 | 0.96 (B's top not seen); A 2 units, B 1 unit |
| overlap A 1, B 0 (also 2/1) | 0.85314, 0.42657, 0.21329 | 0.96 x 8/9: B's top against **air's** index; A 1, B 2 (A's bottom not seen) |
| contain, equal | 0.85333, 0.42666, 0.42666 | A 1, B 1: after leaving B no medium (A's level was replaced) |
| contain A 0, B 1 | 0.96000, 0.12000, 0.96000 | B invisible: A 3 units |
| contain A 1, B 0 (also 2/1) | 0.85436, 0.21359, 0.42718 | A 2 units (A again after B), B 1 |
| overlap A 0, B 1, internal reflection | 0.82741, ... | incoherent stack with F = 0.04, 0.04, 1/9 gives 0.8276: A's bottom (into B) reflects A's own 0.04, not (2-1.5)^2/3.5^2 = 0.0204 (0.842) |
| overlap A 1, B 0, internal reflection | 0.77267, ... | F = 0.04, 1/9, 1/9 gives 0.7742 (B's top from inside A: B's own 1/9) |
| contain A 1, B 0, internal reflection | 0.74957, ... | F = 0.04, 1/9, 1/9, 0.04 gives 0.7500 |

The direction (the image row of an emitter's edge through the same overlap as wide slabs, seen at 45 degrees; offset
along the camera's up axis in units):

| Case | Karma | own index at every surface | ratio of the media's indices |
|---|---|---|---|
| equal | -0.113 | -0.110 | - |
| A 0, B 1 | -0.316 | total internal reflection at B's exit | -0.316 |
| A 1, B 0 | -0.202 | 1.63 | -0.205 |

So Karma's rule, consistent with every case: a path keeps the refracting objects it is inside, **one per priority
level** (0 the highest). Entering an object takes its level's place (an equal priority replaces), leaving an object
empties its level. A surface whose level is below the highest occupied one is a **false interface**: crossed as if
it were not there (no Fresnel, no shading). The interior's absorption is the highest occupied level's. At a real
interface the transmission **bends by the ratio** of the surface's index and the index of the medium on its far side
(the highest occupied level other than its own, or air), but the **reflectance is the surface's own index against
air**. Equal priorities are exactly Karma's behaviour without nesting, which is also Nova's.

Further measurements:

- Opaque objects with a priority inside glass (a black box of priority 1 in a glass of 0): rendered (black), so only
  refracting objects take part.
- Refraction limits (`refractionlimit` 1 and 2): false interfaces are not counted (limit 1, A 0 over B 1 contained:
  not black). Past the limit Karma's thin-wall pass-through (`thinwallatrefractlimit`) still enters or leaves the
  medium, and tints by the transmission colour like a thin-walled surface: contain, equal, limit 2: R 0.85452,
  G 0.21363, B 0.21363 (A 1 + 1 unit, B 1 + 1, nothing after the pass-throughs); A 1, B 0, limit 2: G 0.10685
  (A 3 units: 1 + 1 in A after leaving B + the tint), B 0.21371; A 0, B 1, limit 1: G 0.06000 (A 3 + the tint).
- Shadows (fake caustics, Karma's default) through the stacks: the same with every priority (shadow / lit floor
  0.61316, 0.15365, 0.15374 for equal and A 0, B 1): shadow rays cross every surface. Each crossing of an absorbing
  glass tints by its transmission colour (two crossings of A: green x0.25), not by Beer over the length.

### 1.2 Nova

- **Per path** (`core/integrator/nested.h`, `NestedMedia`, shared by `wavefront.cpp` and `kernels.cu`): up to four
  levels (priority, index, absorption), sorted, highest first. `falseInterface(p)`, `otherIndex(p)`, `enter`, `leave`.
  Glass, liquid, ice and bubbles fit; entering a fifth level drops the lowest.
- **Per object**: `ObjectShading::dielectricPriority` from `nova:object:dielectricPriority` (new, the Render Geometry
  Settings LOP's Nova tab), else the prim's `karma:object:dielectricpriority`, else the render settings' value (Nova's
  or Karma's), else 0 (P1's fallback rule).
- **Only where priorities differ** (`Scene::nestedDielectrics()`: two meshes with different priorities) the
  integrators track the levels; otherwise Nova's medium tracking runs as before (it is the equal-priority rule), and
  the GPU wave does not even hold the levels (72 bytes a path).
- **At a hit** whose closure refracts (a dielectric lobe that transmits, `refractingIndex`, also giving the surface's
  own index): a false interface pushes the path on along its direction (no event, no ray-limit count, no LPE event,
  no emission, the ray cone continues) after entering or leaving the object's level, with the material evaluated for
  its index and absorption. At a real interface `setMediaIndex` gives the dielectric lobes the media's ratio
  (`DielectricLobe::etaMedia`): it bends the transmission (refraction, the generalized half vector, its Jacobian and
  the radiance scale), decides total internal reflection and index matching; the reflectance stays the surface's own
  index, as its outside value at the microfacet angle (no total reflection of its own). A transmission through the
  surface (also index-matched, also a thin-wall pass-through) enters or leaves the level.
- **Thin-wall pass-through at the refraction limit** (both modes, Karma's measured rule): enters or leaves the
  medium like a refraction (Nova kept the medium before) and tints by `ClosureList::absorptionColor`, the colour an
  absorbing interior stands for (1 otherwise: a non-absorbing interior's colour is already the lobe's tint).
- **Fake caustics**: shadow rays and the fake-caustic pass-throughs after a diffuse event cross every surface as
  before (Karma: priorities change no shadow). Karma's fake-caustic factor now also tints absorbing glass by its
  transmission colour per crossing (`fakeCausticFactor`, Karma style only); Nova's physical style is unchanged.
- **Internal reflection** (P1's `internalreflectenable`): unchanged, by the hit's side; Karma's measured
  reflectances at real nested interfaces with it on are the surfaces' own (0.04 into B from inside A).

## 2. Light portals

### 2.1 Karma, measured

A closed 4 x 4 x 3 room of grey walls with a 1.5 x 1 window in its +x wall, lit only by a white constant dome (no
other light); a camera under the ceiling looks down. The portal: a quad in the window with `isportal` on.

| Case | Karma mean | |
|---|---|---|
| no portal | 0.01432 | |
| portal (`portaldomelights` "*") | 0.01431 | the same light |
| the same quad without `isportal` | 0 | an ordinary quad blocks the window |
| flipped quad (normal outwards) | 0.01431 | either side lets light through |
| two windows, no portal | 0.02834 | |
| two windows, portal in one | 0.01417 | **half: the dome lights only through its portals** |
| outside, looking at the window over a ground plane | 0.30520 | the outside wall and ground lit by the dome |
| the same with the portal | 0.00155 | **dark**: only the room seen through the window is lit |
| sun through the window, with / without portal | 0.01593 / 0.01593 | the portal blocks nothing |

Noise (RMS difference of an 8-sample render to the 256-sample portal image): no portal 0.02338, portal 0.00207
(x0.089), `portalmisbias` -1 0.02338 (bit-identical to no portal: environment sampling only), +1 0.00123 (x0.053).
Two domes (red and blue) and two windows, portal in one: `portaldomelights` "*" restricts both, "/World/domeR" only
the red one, "/World/domeB /World/nothing" only the blue one; a bare name ("domeR") and an empty list serve none.

So: an object with `isportal` renders nothing for any ray (camera, shadow, indirect) and turns into openings of the
dome lights its list names (space-separated prim paths, "*" all). A dome with portals shines only along lines through
them: the image mean stays only where every opening the dome lit through has a portal. Next-event estimation samples
the portals, mixed with the dome's own map by `portalmisbias` (-1 map only, +1 portals only). The dome is not
camera-visible in Karma by default; Nova applies the same restriction to camera rays of a visible dome.

### 2.2 Nova

- **Hydra** (`hydra/mesh.cpp` `_SyncPortal`): a mesh with `nova:object:isPortal` (new, Render Geometry Settings Nova
  tab), else `karma:object:isportal`, else the render settings' value, is no scene mesh; its triangles in world space
  (each instance's, at the frame) go to `Scene::setPortal` under the prim's path with its dome list
  (`nova:object:portalDomeLights`, else Karma's, else the render settings', else "*"; string arrays are joined). Lights
  carry their path and `portalMisBias` (`inputs:nova:light:portalMisBias`, else Karma's). Points, curves and spheres
  with `isportal` warn once and render as themselves.
- **Scene** (`commit`): every dome gets the triangles of the portals whose list matches its path (`portalServes`:
  `*` and `?` wildcards), stored with their normals and areas (`PortalTriangle`, `core/lights/shapes.h`);
  `LightData::portals`, `portalCount`, `portalArea`, `portalMix` = (1 + bias) / 2.
- **Sampling** (`samplePortalDome`, `core/lights/lights.h`): with probability `portalMix` (always for a dome without
  an importance map, which Nova otherwise leaves to BSDF sampling) a portal triangle, chosen by the solid angle it
  subtends (by area beyond 16 triangles), and a point on it by `sampleTriangle` (spherical triangle sampling where
  stable); else a direction of the dome's map. Light only where the line from the point crosses a portal; the density
  is the mixture over every portal triangle that line crosses (`domeSamplePdf`, also the MIS density of BSDF rays that
  escape). The shadow ray goes to infinity: the portal is no geometry, the room behind it still occludes.
- **Restriction**: BSDF rays that escape (`escapedRadiance`, from their gather site), camera rays of a visible dome
  (`cameraEnvironment`), the unoccluded LPE estimate and shadow-linked domes' BSDF samples see a portal dome only
  through its portals.
- **Selection**: a portal dome's power is the flux of its mean radiance through the portals (pi L A); in the light
  tree its share is its mean radiance times the solid angle its portals subtend at the receiver
  (`LightTreeView::portalTris`).
- **XPU**: the same headers; the portal triangles and the tree's per-dome ranges are uploaded with the lights.

## 4. Deviations and open items

- Nested dielectrics: per-instance priorities (`instance_override`) are not read: a point instancer's instances take
  their prototype's. Rough interfaces between nested media keep the energy compensation of the surface's own index.
  Four levels per path.
- The reflectance at nested real interfaces is the surface's own index (Karma, measured at normal incidence) as its
  outside Fresnel at the microfacet angle; Karma's angular dependence there was not measured (its Standard Surface
  dielectric is Schlick-like, M3).
- Portals: the portal geometry is taken at the frame (no motion blur); portals on points, curves and spheres are not
  supported (warned). The light tree's per-receiver estimate counts overlapping portal triangles twice.
- Karma's `portalmisbias` mapping onto the mixture probability is Nova's choice ((1 + b) / 2); Karma's measured ends
  (-1: environment only, +1: portals only) agree.
